How the tracker decides

Each child is compared with their own earlier days. The last 7 days are the recent window and every day before that is the usual level. A flag appears when any of these is true:

  • Average active minutes in the recent window fall below 80% of the usual level.
  • Average resting heart rate rises above 110% of the usual level.
  • A symptom shows up on 4 or more of the last 7 days.

These thresholds are design choices for this project, not medical rules, so some false alarms and misses are expected.

About the data. Age mix, murmur rate and BMI patterns come from the CirCor DigiScope pediatric dataset (942 children, Northeast Brazil). The 300 children, their 30 days of logs, the symptoms and the worse weeks are simulated. Symptom lists follow NHLBI, Mayo Clinic, CDC and NHS pages on congenital heart defects.
